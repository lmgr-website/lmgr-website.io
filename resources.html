Resources

The following organizations provide grief, bereavement, counselling, and educational support in the community.

B.C. Bereavement Helpline

A referral service for bereavement resources in British Columbia. The BCBH also runs a number of loss-specific grief groups including those for homicide, suicide, substance abuse, and pet loss.

(604) 738-9950
1-877-779-2223
contact@bcbh.ca

Callanish Society

Offers support programs and retreats for those experiencing cancer and their families.

(604) 732-0633
info@callanish.org

Lumara Grief and Bereavement Care

Offers grief counselling, support groups and camps for families, workshops and supports for health care professionals and others.

1-866-958-6272
info@lumarasociety.org

Vancouver Hospice Society

Offers a number of supportive services, including grief and bereavement groups, individual counselling, walking groups, and healing touch.

(604) 737-7305
administration@vancouverhospice.org
