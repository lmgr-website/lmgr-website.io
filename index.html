Our Mission

Supporting the bereaved in rebuilding their lives by creating safe, compassionate spaces to share and process grief together. We believe in the power of community, empathy, and healing.

Our Values

  • Healing
  • Hope
  • Community
  • Integrity

  • Inclusion

What We Offer

Programs and resources to help you navigate grief

Support Groups

Join a compassionate space to share and heal with others. Our facilitated 6-week groups offer support, tools, and community.

Learn more

Education

Workshops and training for individuals and organizations to better understand grief and provide support to others.

Learn more

Resources
