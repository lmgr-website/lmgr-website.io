About Us

Vancouver Grief Society is a non-profit charitable organization that has been serving the Lower Mainland for 35 years. We offer grief support to those who have experienced the death of a significant person in their lives.

Our programs provide tools and guidance to help you navigate your grief journey in a non-judgmental, caring environment alongside others who understand your experience.

What We Offer

Grief Support Groups

Our support groups provide a safe space to process grief alongside others who understand. Trained facilitators guide participants through healing by offering coping tools and encouraging sharing in a supportive environment.

Grief Education

We offer educational programs for individuals and organizations seeking to develop skills in supporting themselves and others through grief. Learn practical approaches to grief support from our experienced team.

Memorial Services

Honor the memory of your loved ones through our memorial programs. We provide resources and support for creating meaningful tributes and commemorations that celebrate the lives of those we've lost.

Support Our Work

As a non-profit organization that doesn't receive public funding, we rely on the generosity of our community. Your contributions of time and financial support allow us to continue providing these essential services.
