Support Groups

Over the course of six weeks, groups of 6-12 participants meet once per week in two-hour evening sessions. Trained facilitators offer information on grief and bereavement, encouraging participants to learn and confidentially share in a safe, supportive setting.

Our groups offer a three-way approach to those in grief:

  • practical information and tools to help as a guide through loss
  • a chance to connect with others with similar experiences who "get it"
  • a process-oriented approach that includes week-to-week practices
