Support Groups

Groups of 6-12 members meet in two-hour evening sessions once per week. Over the course of six weeks, trained facilitators present information on grief and bereavement, encouraging participants to learn and confidentially share in a safe, supportive setting.

Downtown

October 20 - November 24, 2025

7-9pm

Kerrisdale

October 7 - November 18, 2025

7-9pm

You may register at any time. The intake process for each group will begin roughly one month prior to the start of the session. One of our facilitators will be in touch via email to set up a brief phone conversation to learn more about your loss and provide additional information about the program, ensuring a good fit for your needs.
