About Us

The Vancouver Grief Society is led by individuals who bring personal and professional experience in grief, healing, and community support.

Board of Directors

Ann Caulfield President

Ann’s experience with deep grief began with the loss of her husband in 2010. She has been a facilitator with the society for 10 years. In 2019 Ann joined the board as facilitator liaison and in 2022 was elected president. Ann calls North Vancouver home, where she works as a bookkeeper and administrative assistant.

Morgyn Chandler Secretary

Morgyn has served on the VGS board since 2013. She brings to her legal practice and board work a deep understanding of how loss and grief impact emotional and physical well-being.

Chris Newell Facilitator Liaison

Following the death of a friend to suicide in 2005, Chris began studying loss-related grief and became a facilitator in 2008. She developed the foundation for VGS’s grief education workshops and served as president from 2015–2022.

Karen Parrish Community Relations / Communications

Karen is a writer and poet who lost her husband in 2019. After attending VGS groups, she trained as a facilitator and joined the board in 2024. She lives in Vancouver and enjoys nature walks with her adult children.
